COMMONLY ASKED

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about participating in UGSOT Open Source Events.

Who can attend the events?

Each organizer sets eligibility. Check the official program rules; a sample save on this portal does not establish eligibility or admission.

How do the Digital Hacker Passes work?

The demo generates a sample pass for the selected profile. The code is decorative; real admission requires organizer registration and server-side verification.

Can I participate online or hybrid?

Formats vary. Use each program’s official site to confirm online, hybrid or in-person participation.

How do I become an Admin or Club Lead?

The admin page is a browser-local preview. Real organizer accounts and role assignment require the backend integration described in the deployment guide.
