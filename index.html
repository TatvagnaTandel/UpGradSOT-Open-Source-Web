COMMONLY ASKED

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about participating in UGSOT Open Source Events.

Who can attend the events?

All students of upGrad School of Technology and affiliated college programs are welcome! We have beginner-friendly tracks alongside advanced hacker challenges.

How do the Digital Hacker Passes work?

When you RSVP to an event, an official UGSOT Hacker Pass is generated with your name, student ID, and unique scan code. Show it at the venue entrance to check in.

Can I participate online or hybrid?

Yes! Almost all of our workshops and hackathon keynotes are streamed live on Discord and Zoom with interactive breakout rooms.

How do I become an Admin or Club Lead?

Active contributors who maintain high Dev XP, mentor juniors, or build club tools are invited to join the Core Lead team each semester.
